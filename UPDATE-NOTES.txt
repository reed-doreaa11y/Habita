Habita v66 — Coin repairs, and the completion percentage is fixed at 70%

- The completion percentage is now fixed at 70%. The picker is removed from Settings, and any percentage saved earlier is ignored. Days already counted stay as they were.
- Repair a missed day: on the Calendar, a "Repair a missed day" card appears when yesterday or the day before had habits due and you finished below 70%. Repairing costs 30 coins.
- Rules: one repair per week (the week starts on Sunday); only days that had habits due; only yesterday or the day before; you need 30 coins.
- A repaired day keeps your streak (current streak, best streak and the monthly recap's best run) but earns no coins and does not count as a complete day. It shows highlighted with a dashed border on the Calendar.

Validation: inline JavaScript syntax checked; a missed weekday dropped the streak to 1 and a repair restored it to 11 in a test. Layout of the repair card still needs a check on your phone.

- Streaks: a day with no habits due no longer breaks your streak (current streak, best streak, and the longest run in the monthly recap). It adds nothing and takes nothing. Only a day with habits due that you finished below your percentage breaks it. Coins are unchanged: rest days earn none.
- Today never breaks your streak while it is still in progress.
