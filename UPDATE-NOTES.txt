- New Schedule page: open it from the + menu ("Open schedule") or "Open ›" on the Home card. It has a day strip (dots show days with tasks), tasks split into "At a time" and "Anytime", and a + to add more.
- Tasks: tap the circle to mark done; ✕ cancels that day's occurrence (Undo brings it back). Tap a task to edit or delete it.
- Add/edit form: title, optional time (start/end), date, repeat on chosen days, and "How many times?" to stop a repeating task after N occurrences (empty = keeps repeating), plus a note.
- Home shows a compact "Today's schedule" card with the same check-off.
- Tasks no longer count toward the monthly "best weekday" score, so they have nothing to do with streaks, rings, coins or awards.
- Existing schedule items are kept and now support done/cancel and repeat limits.
- Fixed: bottom sheets sat underneath the bottom bar, which could cover their last buttons. They now sit above it, and the task form's Save row stays visible while scrolling.

Validation: inline JavaScript syntax checked; in a headless browser I created a timed repeating task with a 3-time limit and a one-off task, completed one, cancelled the other, confirmed the limit stops on day 4, confirmed streak and coins did not change, and confirmed Save is tappable.

Habita v110 — Per-goal streak and a tidier goal page

- Each goal page now has a streak card: switch between Daily, Weekly and Monthly (saved per goal). Shows the streak number, the last 7 days / 7 weeks / 6 months as check circles, and Success / Missed / Best cards.
- Daily: a day counts when at least 70% of that goal's habits due are done (days with nothing due are skipped).
- Weekly / Monthly: a week or month counts when you did any of the goal's habits on at least N days; N is set with the − / + under the circles (defaults 3 a week, 12 a month). The current week or month never breaks the streak until it ends below the target.
- Goal page: compact header (icon, name, chips, progress ring), the old stats row and "Last 14 days" strip are replaced by the streak card, and "Mark completed" sits under each habit.
- The overall streak (Progress tab), streak awards and coin repairs are unchanged and still daily.

Validation: tested the streak math on seeded data in all three modes and rendered the goal page in light and dark at phone width. Not tested on a device.

Habita v112 — Start date, and gentler first habits from Bita

- Bita now asks when you want to start (today, tomorrow or a date) before it summarises the goal, and suggests tomorrow if it's already evening. The goal's habits and day 1 begin on that date; nothing shows or counts as missed before it. The goal card in chat shows "Starts <date>" when it isn't today.

- Unchanged: skipping a habit still doesn't count as done toward the 70% rule.

Habita v113 — Bita for projects and deadlines, and Bita remembers and adjusts

- Projects: Bita skips the beginner and course-path questions, asks what the project has to include and what's already done, and builds 3 to 6 steps from what's left (biggest or riskiest first, each with its own target date).
- Several deadlines / "I'm behind": Bita talks it through first (items, due dates, what's done, hours per day), adds up work against time, says honestly if it won't fit, orders by due date, then creates the most urgent goal first and offers the next. For a close deadline it sizes sessions to the days left (longer or more frequent, not more habits) and starts with one concrete step today.
- Learning: when you tell Bita something lasting (free hours, best time of day, what you struggle with), it saves a short note and uses it in later chats. See, remove or clear them in Profile > What Bita remembers. Notes stay on the device (and in the backup export).
- Adjusting: Bita now sees each goal's last-7-day completion and deadline, and offers one adjustment (shorter or fewer sessions, a different time, a re-plan or a later date) when you're falling behind. It only suggests; it never changes habits itself. A new "Adjust my plan" chip starts that chat.
- Unchanged: the weekly plan-review proposals that you approve.

Validation: tested with a stand-in AI that Bita's notes are saved and stripped from the reply, the per-goal numbers and new instructions reach the prompt, and the new Profile page and chip render. Not tested against the live AI or on a device.

Habita v122 — Bita and overload

- Bita now asks once how many hours a week you can give to all your goals together, and saves it (she saves it again if you tell her it changed). It is used for every new goal.

If it doesn't fit, the card shows "This may be too much" with three choices: Make it lighter (lowers the hours and shortens the habits), Start in 2 weeks, or Ease my other goals (Bita reviews your whole plan right after the goal is created). It also asks "Which goal matters most right now?" and Bita won't shorten that goal's habits. - The "Plan your goals" form had no button leading to it, so it was never reachable. It's left in the code but not used; goals are added one at a time with Bita.
