Habita v90 — Automatic roll-over, skip, and a Missed habits chart

- Roll-over is automatic. When the app opens on a new day, any habit that was due yesterday and was not ticked or skipped comes back in a "Carried over" list on Home. There is nothing to tap to make it happen.
- Clashes: a carried habit lands on the first day with no clash. It clashes if the same habit is already due that day, or another habit or carried item is within 45 minutes of its time. If so it moves to the next free day. Tomorrow's own habits are never moved.
- A daily habit clashes with itself every day, so it never carries over: its next normal occurrence is the make-up.
- A carried item that is still not done the next day rolls on again, up to 3 times, then it is recorded as missed.
- Each unfinished habit on Home has a "Skip" button (Undo on the row). A skipped habit is not carried over.
- Carried items have their own check circle and "Drop" link (a dropped item is recorded as skipped). They never change yesterday, today's count, your streak or coins. The 70% rule is unchanged, and yesterday stays "not done".
- Progress (all goals and each goal page): new "Missed habits" card with Done, Carried over, Skipped and No answer, how many carried habits were made up, and the three most often missed habits for the chosen range.
- Old data loads fine.

Validation: inline JavaScript syntax checked; headless-browser tests seeded habits and confirmed: a missed habit carries to today, a clashing one moves to tomorrow, daily, skipped and done habits do not carry, nothing is created twice after a reload, a pending item rolls and then expires after 3 rolls, and streak and coins are unaffected. Habita v113 — Bita for projects and deadlines, and Bita remembers and adjusts

- Projects: Bita skips the beginner and course-path questions, asks what the project has to include and what's already done, and builds 3 to 6 steps from what's left (biggest or riskiest first, each with its own target date).
